The cycle

The book runs on a 21-session clock. Every 21st trading day is a rebalance day: the engine ranks the universe at that day's close and executes all resulting orders at the next morning's open (never the same close — that fill wouldn't be executable). Between rebalances, only one thing runs: a daily volatility guard. There is no discretionary input anywhere.

  1. Universe & hard gates

    Starting from ~2,000 NSE symbols with survivorship-aware history, a stock is a candidate on rebalance day only if it passes all five gates on that day's indicators: 1-month average turnover ≥ ₹8 Cr (liquidity), close above its own SMA200 (long-term uptrend), close ≥ ₹20 (no penny stocks), IFP quality score ≥ 0.38, and daily ATR ≤ 5% of price (volatility ceiling at entry). Typically 150–400 names survive.

  2. Composite ranking

    Survivors are scored against each other on that day only (cross-sectional z-scores — no look-ahead): z(12-1m mom) + z(6m mom) + z(3m mom) + z(6m/ATR) + z(−20d base range), each weighted 1.0. The fourth term is momentum per unit of volatility; the fifth rewards tight recent consolidation. Highest score first: the top 60 form the buffer, the top 30 the target book.

  3. Sells — rank hysteresis

    A holding is kept as long as it stays anywhere in the top-60 buffer. It is sold at next open (RANK_DROP) only when it falls out of the top 60 or closes below its SMA200 (which removes it from the ranked set by construction). The 2× buffer is deliberate hysteresis — it stops the book churning names that hover around rank 30.

  4. Capital & compounding

    Sizing capital = clamp(₹4L + realized P&L, floor ₹4L, cap ₹2 Cr) — profit-only compounding: wins scale future positions, but sizing never drops below starting capital in a drawdown, and stops scaling at ₹2 Cr. Realized P&L flows back in at the next rebalance, which is what turns a ~17% average year into geometric growth.

  5. Position sizing — inverse volatility

    Free slots = 30 − current holdings, filled from the top of the ranking. Each buy is weighted by 1/ATR%, normalized so the full 30-slot book sums to 100% — a 2%-ATR largecap gets roughly triple the capital of a 6%-ATR midcap, equalizing risk per position instead of rupees. Partial fills are scaled down so the book is never levered.

  6. Entry execution

    All buys fill at next morning's open +0.20% slippage, with the full Dhan delivery cost model (STT 0.1%, stamp 0.015%, exchange 0.003%, DP ₹14.75 per sell, zero brokerage). The invalidation reference is the distance to SMA200 — there is no hard stop-loss in this book; exits come from rank, trend, or the volatility guard.

  7. Daily guard — the one divergence

    #909: if a holding's ATR exceeds 5% for 2 consecutive sessions, the whole position exits at next open. One noisy day is ignored; a persistent volatility regime change is not.

    The aggressive preset (#1062) changes this rule: exit only when ATR expands above 1.5× its own 20-session average and the close is below the EMA21 — so a stock getting volatile while rallying is left alone — and even then only 33% is trimmed. It also adds breadth-smile sizing: when 45–60% of the liquid universe is above its 200-SMA (the historical "chop band", worst forward returns), new-entry exposure is halved; at washout (<30%, historically the best forward-return state: +8.8%/quarter, 89% win) and in healthy trends (≥60%) the book runs full size.

  8. Combo additions (the balanced preset, #1079)

    Three further mechanisms, each split-validated 2026-08-21, stack on top of the aggressive config: a 52-week-high factor (George & Hwang 2004 — nearness to the 52-week high joins the composite at weight 1.0), an earnings entry gate (skip any buy whose company reports results within 14 days of the fill — the gap-risk a monthly book cannot exit fast enough to dodge), and 6% annual yield on idle cash (liquid-fund parking, previously modeled at 0%). Net effect: +2.9pt CAGR and −1pt MaxDD over the aggressive preset, with the weak-regime window (2011–18) improving from 12.9%/20.5% to 16.0%/18.6%. Trade-off to know: in the strong 2019–26 regime it gives back ~2pt CAGR vs the pure aggressive config — it rebalances performance across regimes rather than dominating everywhere. Its earnings gate needs the filings harvest kept current (coverage thins after 2024).

  9. Loop

    Sale proceeds and realized P&L return to the capital base; 21 sessions later the ranking runs again. Over 2011–2026 this cycles ~185 times and produces ~2,300 round trips.
